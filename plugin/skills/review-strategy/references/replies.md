# Writing review replies

Replies are drafts. The owner reads, edits and posts each one; you never post anything.

## Every reply

- Short: two to four sentences. Thank the reviewer by the name Google shows.
- Answer what they wrote. Name the service or visit they mention, never a generic line that
  could sit under any review.
- Sign off as the business or the owner by first name if the user gives one.
- No keywords stuffed in ("best emergency plumber in West Nyack"): readers notice and it does
  nothing for ranking.

## Negative reviews

- Acknowledge the problem without arguing the facts in public. Never call the reviewer a liar.
- Never confirm private details: that someone is a patient or client, their address, what they
  paid. Health, legal and financial businesses have rules on this.
- Offer one way to talk offline: a named person and a phone number or email.
- If the review looks fake (no record of the customer, a competitor, an obvious pattern), do not
  say so in the reply. Draft a neutral reply and note for the owner that they can flag it in the
  Business Profile, with the reason. Spam evidence belongs in the `spam-fighter` skill.

## Positive reviews

- Vary the wording; twenty identical replies read as automated.
- Mention something specific from the review and, where natural, invite them back.
