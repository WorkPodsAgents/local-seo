# Single purpose

For the **Privacy practices** tab, field **Single purpose description**:

> Local SEO audits a local business's Google Business Profile and its visibility in Google Maps:
> it reads the profile, reviews, posts and Maps search results the user opens, checks the
> business's own website against the profile, and shows the user what to fix. Every feature
> (the profile audit, the Maps rankings, the review analysis, the website check and the AI answer
> check) serves that one audit, from the side panel or from the user's own AI agent through a local
> connection.
